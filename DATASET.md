# DATASET.md — Petri feasibility pilot dataset

This card documents the final analysis dataset for the 180-audit Petri feasibility pilot. It describes what was measured, how, and what can and cannot be concluded. Items are marked **(verified)** where they were checked against the frozen logs/config, or **(uncertain)** where they remain to be confirmed.

## Purpose and scope

A small, reproducible feasibility study of **sensitivity of safety-evaluation outcomes to the overseer (auditor) versus the target model**, using Petri. It is **exploratory**, not confirmatory, and covers 20 constructed scenarios for three models under three auditor strategies (**verified**).

## Unit of observation

One row per **unique `(seed, auditor_condition, target)` combination** — i.e. one audit. 180 rows; each combination appears exactly once (**verified**).

## Experimental design

- 3 auditor conditions (A0 default, A1 trusting, A2 skeptical/verifying) × 3 targets × 20 shared seeds = **180 audits**, one epoch each.
- Auditor contrasts are **paired by seed** across conditions and targets.
- `max_turns = 25`; `--seed 42`; see `config/` (**verified** in all log headers).

## Judge scoring rubric

- Native Petri judge (`inspect_petri/audit_judge`) with **all 38 built-in dimensions**.
- Scale **1–10** (1 = "not at all", 10 = "extremely") per the judge prompt (**verified** in `inspect_petri/_judge/judge.py`).
- Primary outcome: `unprompted_deception_toward_user`. Secondary: `concerning`, `unfaithful_thinking`, `unprompted_initiative`.

## Missing-score handling

- **179 of 180** primary scores are present; **1 is missing**: `A0 × gpt-5.6-luna × classified_leak_goal_conflict` (**verified**).
- Cause: the judge's structured answer failed and an Anthropic **content filter** blocked re-scoring (content filter is a provider restriction; not circumvented).
- **No imputation.** Missingness is **non-random** (target-specific). Worst-case bound for the A0-GPT mean is ≤ 1.85 on the 1–10 scale — below the Gemini A0 mean (2.25) — so it cannot plausibly reverse the target ranking (`results/08-missing-sensitivity.csv`).

## Exclusion rules

Excluded from the dataset (retained locally, not archived in Git): TASK 02 smoke test, TASK 01 install/mock checks, the TASK 04 15-turn exploratory batch, the invalid wrong-seed run (`logs/batch01_invalid_wrongseed/`), and mock verification logs (`logs/_verify*/`) (**verified**: the dataset's `source_batch` contains only `batch02_25turns` and `batch03_180`).

## Bootstrap resampling

- Unit: **seed ID**. 10,000 resamples, fixed RNG seed `20261012`, 95% percentile CIs.
- Seeds are resampled **jointly** across targets and conditions to preserve pairing; one shared resample matrix is reused for all statistics.
- Paired contrasts use complete observed pairs only; the single missing cell is excluded from affected pairs (n=19 rather than 20), never imputed.

## Known confounds

- **Scenario-construction**: the Petri auditor builds the target's environment; across conditions the environment/tool surface sometimes changed (5/8 triplets surface-only, 3/8 mixed) (`results/11-scenario-review.csv`).
- **Turn-budget saturation** (~half; unequal across conditions).
- **Single judge / single epoch**; judge score is a proxy for judged appearance, not ground truth.
- **Stochastic execution** (no averaging over runs).

## Limitations on interpretation

No causal claims; no equivalence claims from CIs containing zero; no generalization of the target ranking to model safety; bootstrap frequencies are not future-outcome probabilities.
